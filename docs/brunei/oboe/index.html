About Allah Peliharakan Sultan

Written by two young Bruneians in 1947 and adopted in 1951, "Allah Peliharakan Sultan" became the anthem of the independent state in 1984. It is a prayer for the Sultan, asking God to protect the ruler and to keep the country and its people in peace.

About this orchestral arrangement

A royal procession: slow and stately, the tune passes from violins to trumpets, and the choir enters for the sung pass, ending in a broad, ceremonial finale.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F major, 4/4, 2:33, 16 parts.

Who wrote Allah Peliharakan Sultan?

The music is by Awang Haji Besar Sagap (1947) and the words by Pengiran Haji Mohamed Yusuf (1947). It was adopted 1951; national anthem of independent Brunei since 1984.

Is the melody the official one?
