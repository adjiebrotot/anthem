About La Brabançonne

"La Brabançonne" was written in Brussels during the Belgian Revolution of 1830 that led to independence from the Netherlands. The music is by the opera singer François Van Campenhout; the words, rewritten by Charles Rogier in 1860, call for unity under "Le Roi, la Loi, la Liberté" (the King, the Law, Freedom). It is sung in Belgium's three national languages.

About this orchestral arrangement

Carillon and brass band: a bell-like intro of harp and plucked strings, like the tower bells of Flemish towns, then a confident festive march led by trumpets as cornets and horns in a warm, euphonium-like band colour.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Bb major, 4/4, 1:09, 19 parts.
