About Bahrainona

"Bahrainona" (Our Bahrain) has been the anthem of Bahrain since independence in 1971. Its words, revised in 2002 when the country became a kingdom, salute Bahrain as a land of peace, an Arab nation and a kingdom of the honest, under the rule of its king and its constitution.

About this orchestral arrangement

A Gulf pageant: ceremonial and bright, with an oboe carrying an Arabic-flavoured melodic colour, trumpet fanfares and the strings in unison over a steady processional pulse.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F major, 4/4, 1:17, 18 parts.
