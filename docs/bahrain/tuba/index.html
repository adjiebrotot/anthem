About Bahrainona

"Bahrainona" (Our Bahrain) has been the anthem of Bahrain since independence in 1971. Its words, revised in 2002 when the country became a kingdom, salute Bahrain as a land of peace, an Arab nation and a kingdom of the honest, under the rule of its king and its constitution.

About this orchestral arrangement

A Gulf pageant: ceremonial and bright, with an oboe carrying an Arabic-flavoured melodic colour, trumpet fanfares and the strings in unison over a steady processional pulse.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F major, 4/4, 1:17, 18 parts.

Can I use this arrangement of Bahrainona, and how do I credit it?

The melody of Bahrainona is still in copyright and belongs to its rights holders in Bahrain; using it follows their rules. That usually means it is fine at meetings, educational events, cultural ceremonies and social activities, but never in a context that defames it, and never commercially without their permission. Who wrote Bahrainona?

The music is by adopted in 1971 (composer not officially credited) and the words by Mohamed Sudqi Ayyash (current text 2002). It was adopted 1971 (music); words revised 2002.
