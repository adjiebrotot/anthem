About Ja, vi elsker dette landet

Bjørnstjerne Bjørnson, Nobel laureate in literature, wrote the poem and his cousin Rikard Nordraak set it to music; it was first sung publicly on Constitution Day, 17 May 1864. It loves the land rising "furet, værbitt over vannet", furrowed and weather-beaten above the sea. The Storting made its status official in 2019.

About this orchestral arrangement

Fjord grandeur: broad low strings, deep horns and low brass, building to a powerful brass climax with cymbal.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Bb major, 4/4, 1:22, 18 parts.

The music is by Rikard Nordraak (1864) and the words by Bjørnstjerne Bjørnson (1859-1868). It was adopted by tradition; confirmed by parliament in 2019.
