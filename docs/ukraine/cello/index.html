About Shche ne vmerla Ukrainy

The poem by the ethnographer Pavlo Chubynskyi (1862) was set to music by the priest and composer Mykhailo Verbytskyi in 1863. The music became the state anthem in 1992 and the words, slightly changed, in 2003. "Душу й тіло ми положим за нашу свободу": we will lay down soul and body for our freedom.

About this orchestral arrangement

Steppe resilience: a cello lament, a growing strength in the strings and horns, and a choir-heavy refrain.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F# minor, 4/4, 1:52, 16 parts.

Who wrote Shche ne vmerla Ukrainy?

The music is by Mykhailo Verbytskyi (1863) and the words by Pavlo Chubynskyi (1862). It was adopted music 1992, words 2003.

What inspired the orchestration?

Steppe resilience: a cello lament, a growing strength in the strings and horns, and a choir-heavy refrain.
