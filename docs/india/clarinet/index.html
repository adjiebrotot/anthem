About Jana Gana Mana

Rabindranath Tagore, Asia's first Nobel laureate, wrote and composed "Jana Gana Mana" in 1911; it was first sung at the Calcutta session of the Indian National Congress on 27 December 1911. The Constituent Assembly adopted its first stanza as the national anthem on 24 January 1950. It names the regions, rivers and mountains of India and ends in "Jaya he", victory.

About this orchestral arrangement

From a dawn raga to an epic finale: a drone like a tanpura in the low strings, a slow flute melody in the middle, and a full orchestra with choir and trumpets for the closing "Jaya he, jaya he".

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Eb major, 4/4, 1:23, 18 parts.
