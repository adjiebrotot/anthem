About Schweizerpsalm

The Cistercian monk Alberich Zwyssig set Leonhard Widmer's poem in 1841, adapting a hymn tune he had composed for the mass. It became the provisional anthem in 1961 and the official one in 1981, sung in Switzerland's four languages. It sees God in the morning glow over the Alps: "Trittst im Morgenrot daher".

About this orchestral arrangement

Alpine dawn: an alphorn-like horn call, a clarinet chorale, and a hymn that grows into a brass and choir finale.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Bb major, 3/4, 1:31, 19 parts.
