About Phleng Chat Thai

Composed by Phra Chenduriyang in 1932, the year Siam became a constitutional monarchy, the anthem got its current words by Luang Saranupraphan in 1939, when Siam became Thailand. It is played every day at 8 a.m. and 6 p.m. across the country. The words speak of a land united in blood and flesh, peace-loving but ready to defend its freedom.

About this orchestral arrangement

A royal march fanfare: crisp and ceremonial, with trumpets and trombones, clarinets, a glitter of harp, and the choir for the last line.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. C major, 4/4, 1:00, 22 parts.

Who wrote Phleng Chat Thai?

The music is by Phra Chenduriyang (Peter Feit) (1932) and the words by Luang Saranupraphan (1939). It was adopted 1932 (music), current words 1939.
