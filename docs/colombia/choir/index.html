About Himno Nacional de la República de Colombia

The poem by Rafael Núñez, later President of Colombia, was set to music by the Italian-born tenor Oreste Síndici and first performed in Bogotá on 11 November 1887; it became the official anthem by law in 1920. The chorus "¡Oh gloria inmarcesible! ¡Oh júbilo inmortal!" celebrates the unfading glory of independence.

About this orchestral arrangement

A heroic choral opera in contrasts: a brass fanfare and a grand chorus of choir and trumpets, then an intimate stanza for cellos and bassoons in thirds over a plucked lilt, and a bigger final chorus.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Eb major (Ab in the stanza's first half), 4/4, 2:29, 19 parts.
