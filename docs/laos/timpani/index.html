About Pheng Xat Lao

Composed by Thongdy Sounthonevichit in 1941, the melody was adopted as the anthem of the Kingdom of Laos in 1947. When the Lao People's Democratic Republic was founded in 1975, Sisana Sisane wrote the new words that are sung today, about the unity and independence of the Lao people.

About this orchestral arrangement

From a river morning to a parade: a pentatonic opening for flute, clarinets and oboe, strings joining, then a march that builds with trumpets, flutes and choir.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F major, 4/4, 2:06, 23 parts.

Who wrote Pheng Xat Lao?

The music is by Thongdy Sounthonevichit (1941) and the words by Sisana Sisane (1975). It was adopted 1947 (music), current words 1975.

What inspired the orchestration?
