About Hino Nacional Brasileiro

The music was composed by Francisco Manuel da Silva in 1831; the words by Joaquim Osório Duque-Estrada were made official in 1922, the centenary of independence. It opens on the banks of the Ipiranga stream, where independence was proclaimed in 1822, and praises a giant land, "gigante pela própria natureza", under the Southern Cross. Both parts are sung in the full anthem.

About this orchestral arrangement

An operatic piano concerto: the piano leads the famous fanfare and runs in arpeggios, the violins race, woodwinds chatter, and a samba lift of tambourine and triangle sits under the lyrical lines before a grand tutti close.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F major, 4/4, 3:11, 18 parts.
