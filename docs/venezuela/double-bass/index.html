About Gloria al Bravo Pueblo

Written in Caracas around 1810, at the start of the independence movement, "Gloria al Bravo Pueblo" (Glory to the brave people who threw off the yoke) was decreed the national anthem on 25 May 1881. Its words, by Vicente Salias, celebrate the law, virtue and honour.

About this orchestral arrangement

The llanero plains: a busy concert harp in the rhythm of the joropo, a plucked bass and a bright triangle pulse, a heroic brass chorus, and a lighter stanza for woodwinds and strings.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. A major, 2/4, 1:35, 19 parts.
