About Deșteaptă-te, române!

Andrei Mureșanu's poem "Un răsunet" was written during the revolution of 1848 and sung to a melody collected by Anton Pann. Sung in the streets during the 1989 revolution, it became the national anthem in 1990. It calls the Romanian to wake from the deadly sleep of oppression. This version plays four stanzas, as on official occasions.

About this orchestral arrangement

The awakening: a dark start for violas and cellos, four stanzas that build one by one with horns, choir and trumpets, to a powerful last stanza.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. E minor, 4/4, 5:07, 21 parts.

Who wrote Deșteaptă-te, române!?

The music is by Anton Pann (1848) and the words by Andrei Mureșanu (1848). It was adopted 1990.

What inspired the orchestration?
