About Advance Australia Fair

Written by Scottish-born composer Peter Dodds McCormick and first performed in Sydney in 1878, "Advance Australia Fair" replaced "God Save the Queen" as the national anthem in 1984, after a 1977 national poll chose it. The words praise a young land of golden soil and wealth for toil; since 2021 the first verse sings "for we are one and free" in recognition of the First Nations.

About this orchestral arrangement

Inspired by wide open landscapes: a rising horn call over string runs opens a light first verse of flutes, clarinets and plucked strings. The second verse fills out with choir and a steady string drive, and the last refrain puts horns and trumpets on the tune in unison.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. G major, 4/4, 2:21, 20 parts.
