About Du gamla, du fria

Richard Dybeck wrote the words in 1844 to a folk melody from Västmanland. Though never adopted by law, it has been Sweden's national anthem by custom since the late 1800s. It hails the ancient, free, mountainous North and its sun, sky and green meadows.

About this orchestral arrangement

Midsummer folk: simple and warm, a flute and clarinets over plucked strings, ending in an airy tutti.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Bb major, 4/4, 1:20, 21 parts.

Who wrote Du gamla, du fria?

The music is by a folk melody from Västmanland and the words by Richard Dybeck (1844). It was adopted by tradition (never fixed by law).

Is the melody the official one?

Yes. The tune is the official melody, note for note, checked against published scores; only the orchestration is new.
