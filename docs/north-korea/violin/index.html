About Aegukka

"Aegukka" (The Patriotic Song) was written in 1947 by the poet Pak Se-yong and the composer Kim Won-gyun, and has been the anthem of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea since its founding. It opens with the morning sun shining on the mountains and rivers of the land.

About this orchestral arrangement

A mountain sunrise: a low horn call and a slow, grand build in sweeping strings, then a broad choir and horns.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Bb major, 4/4, 1:43, 15 parts.

Who wrote Aegukka?

The music is by Kim Won-gyun (1947) and the words by Pak Se-yong (1947). It was adopted 1947.

What inspired the orchestration?
