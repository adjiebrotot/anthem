About Aegukga

The words of "Aegukga" date from the years around 1900 and were long sung to the Scottish tune of "Auld Lang Syne"; Ahn Eak-tai composed the present melody in 1935 as part of his "Korea Fantasia". It was adopted with the founding of the Republic of Korea in 1948. It opens "Until the East Sea's waters and Baekdu Mountain are dry and worn away".

About this orchestral arrangement

Morning calm: a transparent flute and harp opening, the refrain "무궁화 삼천리" taken by choir and horns, and a full last line.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. A major, 4/4, 1:20, 20 parts.

The music is by Ahn Eak-tai (1935) and the words by unknown (late 1890s to 1900s; attributed to Yun Chi-ho or Ahn Chang-ho). It was adopted 1948.
