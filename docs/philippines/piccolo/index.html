About Lupang Hinirang

Julián Felipe's "Marcha Nacional Filipina" was first played at the proclamation of independence on 12 June 1898. José Palma's Spanish poem "Filipinas" gave it words in 1899, and the Filipino text "Lupang Hinirang" (Chosen Land) was adopted in 1956. It ends with a vow: to die for you is a joy.

About this orchestral arrangement

An islands march: a stately band march with trumpets and flutes, lifting from 96 to 124 beats per minute into a fervent choral finale.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. G major, 2/4, 1:16, 17 parts.

Who wrote Lupang Hinirang?

The music is by Julián Felipe (1898) and the words by José Palma (Spanish, 1899); Filipino text 1956, revised 1960s. It was adopted 12 June 1898 (music); Republic Act 8491 (1998).

Is the melody the official one?

Yes. The tune is the official melody, note for note, checked against published scores; only the orchestration is new.

What key and tempo is it in?
