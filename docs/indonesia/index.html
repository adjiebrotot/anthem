About Indonesia Raya

W. R. Supratman first played "Indonesia Raya" on his violin at the Second Youth Congress in Batavia on 28 October 1928, the day of the Youth Pledge (Sumpah Pemuda): one homeland, one nation, one language. It became the anthem of the independence proclaimed in 1945. The refrain "Indonesia Raya, merdeka, merdeka" calls for a great and free Indonesia.

About this orchestral arrangement

A heroic film-score epic with the colours of Indonesia: a gamelan-like interlocking ostinato of harp, pizzicato strings and piano over a gong, a kendang-like hand-drum pulse under horns, then a marching tutti with choir and trumpets on "Indonesia Raya, merdeka, merdeka".

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. G major, 4/4, 1:52, 24 parts.

Who wrote Indonesia Raya?

The music is by Wage Rudolf Supratman (1928) and the words by Wage Rudolf Supratman (1928). It was adopted sung at the Youth Pledge congress 1928; national anthem from independence (1945), official score 1958.

What inspired the orchestration?

A heroic film-score epic with the colours of Indonesia: a gamelan-like interlocking ostinato of harp, pizzicato strings and piano over a gong, a kendang-like hand-drum pulse under horns, then a marching tutti with choir and trumpets on "Indonesia Raya, merdeka, merdeka".

What key and tempo is it in?

This arrangement is in G major, 4/4 and starts at about 104 beats per minute; it lasts 1:52.
