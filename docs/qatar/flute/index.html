About As-Salam al-Amiri

Qatar's anthem was adopted in 1996, when Sheikh Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani became Emir. The words by Sheikh Mubarak bin Saif Al Thani swear by the One who raised the sky and spread the light to stay true to Qatar, guided by the spirit of the forefathers.

About this orchestral arrangement

Epic and ceremonial: a trumpet fanfare, a violin solo passage (played by the violin section) before the upbeat part, and a full closing pass with choir.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. G major, 4/4, 1:36, 19 parts.
