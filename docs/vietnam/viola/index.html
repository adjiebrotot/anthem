About Tiến Quân Ca

Văn Cao wrote "Tiến Quân Ca" (Army March) in 1944; it was sung at the declaration of independence in 1945, became the anthem of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam in 1946, and of unified Vietnam in 1976. Only the first verse is sung: "Đoàn quân Việt Nam đi", the Vietnamese army marches on.

About this orchestral arrangement

Marching forward: a plucked string march under timpani, horn calls and violins, quick bouncing eighths, and a brass chorus. No snare drum.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Bb major, 4/4, 0:57, 13 parts.
