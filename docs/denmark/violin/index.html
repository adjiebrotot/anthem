About Der er et yndigt land

Denmark has two national anthems: the royal "Kong Christian stod ved højen mast" and this civil one. Adam Oehlenschläger's 1819 poem pictures a lovely land of broad beeches by the salt eastern shore, "Gamle Danmark"; Hans Ernst Krøyer's melody from about 1835 is the one sung today.

About this orchestral arrangement

A pastoral coast: the oboes in a gentle solo line, soft woodwinds and strings, light as a summer morning, gathering into a warm full close.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. D major, 4/4, 1:49, 17 parts.

Who wrote Der er et yndigt land?

The music is by Hans Ernst Krøyer (c. 1835) and the words by Adam Oehlenschläger (1819). It was adopted by tradition (Denmark's civil national anthem).

What inspired the orchestration?
