About Marcha Real

The Marcha Real is one of the oldest national anthems in Europe: it appears in a 1761 book of Spanish military bugle calls as the "Marcha Granadera", and Charles III made it the official honour march in 1770. It is one of the few national anthems with no official lyrics, so it is always played as an instrumental.

About this orchestral arrangement

A royal fanfare: trumpets and timpani in grandeur, a soft woodwind middle, and a big brass ending.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. C major, 4/4, 1:29, 19 parts.
