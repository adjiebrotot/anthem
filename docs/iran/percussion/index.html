About Sorud-e Melli-ye Jomhuri-ye Eslami-ye Iran

Iran's current national anthem was adopted in 1990, with music by Hassan Riyahi. It opens with the eastern sun rising on the horizon, and speaks of independence, freedom and the lasting message of the nation.

About this orchestral arrangement

A solemn dawn: restrained and colourful, with oboe and clarinet over a shimmer of tremolo strings, a fanfare, and a climax for choir and trumpets.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F# major, 4/4, 2:15, 22 parts.
