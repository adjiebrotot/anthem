About State Anthem of the Russian Federation

Alexander Alexandrov's melody was written for the Bolshevik Party anthem in 1939 and became the State Anthem of the Soviet Union in 1944. Russia restored it in 2000 with new words by Sergey Mikhalkov, who had co-written the Soviet text: "Rossiya - svyashchennaya nasha derzhava" (Russia, our sacred state). This version sings the first two stanzas, each with the refrain "Slav'sya, Otechestvo".

About this orchestral arrangement

A grand Russian Romantic epic in the spirit of Tchaikovsky, Rachmaninoff and Mussorgsky: tolling bells of low piano and harp octaves with a slow horn chorale, a deep men's choir colour with the cellos, then brass on the refrain under a sweeping string countermelody, and a final refrain with bells on every downbeat.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. C major, 4/4, 2:40, 21 parts.

Who wrote State Anthem of the Russian Federation?

The music is by Alexander Alexandrov (1939/1944) and the words by Sergey Mikhalkov (2000). It was adopted Federal Constitutional Law of 25 December 2000 (music); words approved 30 December 2000.

What inspired the orchestration?

A grand Russian Romantic epic in the spirit of Tchaikovsky, Rachmaninoff and Mussorgsky: tolling bells of low piano and harp octaves with a slow horn chorale, a deep men's choir colour with the cellos, then brass on the refrain under a sweeping string countermelody, and a final refrain with bells on every downbeat.

What key and tempo is it in?

This arrangement is in C major, 4/4 and starts at about 62 beats per minute; it lasts 2:40.
