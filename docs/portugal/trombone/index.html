About A Portuguesa

Written in 1890 as a patriotic answer to the British Ultimatum over Portugal's African colonies, "A Portuguesa" became the national anthem of the Republic in 1911. "Heróis do mar, nobre povo" (heroes of the sea, noble people) recalls the age of discovery; its refrain calls "Às armas!".

About this orchestral arrangement

An Atlantic voyage: surging string waves, heroic horns, and the refrain "Às armas" as a triumphant march for trumpets.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Eb major, 4/4, 1:19, 16 parts.

Who wrote A Portuguesa?

The music is by Alfredo Keil (1890) and the words by Henrique Lopes de Mendonça (1890). It was adopted 1911 (official version 1957).
