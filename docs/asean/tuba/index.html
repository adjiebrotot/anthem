About The ASEAN Way

"The ASEAN Way" is the official anthem of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations, the ten-member bloc of Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam. Composed by two Thai musicians, it was selected in 2008 from entries across the region. Its words, "one vision, one identity, one community", celebrate unity in diversity.

About this orchestral arrangement

Arranged like a cinematic pop anthem: a piano soloist in flowing arpeggios, warm strings and a soft percussion and timpani pulse. The second chorus lifts by a key change into a full finale with choir and trumpets.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. D major, lift to E, 4/4, 1:52, 19 parts.

Who wrote The ASEAN Way?

The music is by Kittikhun Sodprasert and Sampow Triudom and the words by Payom Valaiphatchra. It was adopted 2008, chosen in a region-wide competition.

What inspired the orchestration?

How does The ASEAN Way compare with the other anthems here?
