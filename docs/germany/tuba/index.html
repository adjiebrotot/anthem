About Das Lied der Deutschen

Joseph Haydn composed the melody in 1797 as the Emperor's Hymn for Francis II, and used it again in his "Emperor" String Quartet. Hoffmann von Fallersleben wrote "Das Lied der Deutschen" to it in 1841 on the island of Heligoland. Only the third stanza, "Einigkeit und Recht und Freiheit" (unity and justice and freedom), is the national anthem.

About this orchestral arrangement

From Haydn's quartet to a cathedral: it begins with the strings alone, a nod to the "Emperor" Quartet, passes to horns and woodwinds, and ends as a brass-and-choir chorale.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Eb major, 4/4, 1:14, 19 parts.

Who wrote Das Lied der Deutschen?

The music is by Joseph Haydn (1797) and the words by August Heinrich Hoffmann von Fallersleben (1841). It was adopted third stanza: 1952 (West Germany), 1991 (united Germany).

Is the melody the official one?
