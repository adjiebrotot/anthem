About Maamme

The poem by Finland's national poet J. L. Runeberg opens his "Tales of Ensign Stål"; Fredrik Pacius set it to music and it was first sung in Helsinki on 13 May 1848. Never fixed by law, it is the anthem by tradition, sung in Finnish as "Maamme" and in Swedish as "Vårt land". It praises the poor but beloved northern homeland, its lakes and its forests.

About this orchestral arrangement

A Nordic chorale: plain, cool and hymn-like, the choir carries the tune with horns and trumpets, and the last stanza grows into a broad, glowing close.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. A major, 3/4, 2:06, 15 parts.

Who wrote Maamme?

The music is by Fredrik Pacius (1848) and the words by Johan Ludvig Runeberg (1846, in Swedish). It was adopted by tradition since 1848.

Is the melody the official one?

Yes. The tune is the official melody, note for note, checked against published scores; only the orchestration is new.

What key and tempo is it in?

This arrangement is in A major, 3/4 and starts at about 58 beats per minute; it lasts 2:06.
