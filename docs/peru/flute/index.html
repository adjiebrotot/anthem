About Himno Nacional del Perú

Chosen in a competition called by José de San Martín after the declaration of independence in 1821, the anthem by José Bernardo Alzedo (music) and José de la Torre Ugarte (words) opens "Somos libres, seámoslo siempre": we are free, let us always be so.

About this orchestral arrangement

The Andes: flutes in parallel thirds like panpipes, a harp ostinato, a low-string drone with heavy timpani and bass drum, a thin modal stanza, and a massive last chorus.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F major (stanza F minor / Ab), 4/4, 2:57, 15 parts.

Who wrote Himno Nacional del Perú?

The music is by José Bernardo Alzedo (1821) and the words by José de la Torre Ugarte (1821). It was adopted 1821.
