About Il Canto degli Italiani

Written in Genoa in 1847 by the 20-year-old student Goffredo Mameli and set to music by Michele Novaro, "Fratelli d'Italia" (Brothers of Italy) became the song of the Risorgimento. It was chosen as the provisional anthem of the Republic in 1946 and made official by law in 2017. Its refrain is "Stringiamoci a coorte, siam pronti alla morte".

About this orchestral arrangement

An opera overture: Rossini-bright clarinets and flutes over an oom-pah brass band, a crescendo, and a brilliant refrain.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Bb major, 4/4, 1:14, 17 parts.

Who wrote Il Canto degli Italiani?

The music is by Michele Novaro (1847) and the words by Goffredo Mameli (1847). It was adopted provisionally 1946, by law 2017.
