About Mongol Ulsyn Töriin Duulal

The music, composed by Bilegiin Damdinsüren and Luvsanjambyn Mördorj, has been Mongolia's anthem since 1950. The words by the poet Tsendiin Damdinsüren were revised in 1991 and 2006 and praise the independent, sacred homeland of all Mongols and the legacy of Chinggis Khaan.

About this orchestral arrangement

Steppe horsemen: an open-fifth drone and a three-note hoofbeat in timpani, cellos and basses, the cellos and then the horns on the tune, and a refrain that gallops into a full tutti with choir.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Ab major, 4/4, 0:57, 14 parts.

The music is by Bilegiin Damdinsüren and Luvsanjambyn Mördorj (1950) and the words by Tsendiin Damdinsüren (1950), revised 2006. It was adopted 1950; current words 2006.
