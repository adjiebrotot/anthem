About Ishy Bilady

The melody by the Egyptian composer Mohamed Abdel Wahab was adopted at the founding of the United Arab Emirates in 1971; Arif Al Sheikh Abdullah Al Hassan's words followed in 1986. "Ishy bilady", live my country, it sings, the unity of our Emirates has lived.

About this orchestral arrangement

Epic and cinematic: a piano solo before the full part, violins on the tune, then trumpets and choir.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F major, 4/4, 1:30, 17 parts.
