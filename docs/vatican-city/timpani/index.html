About Inno e Marcia Pontificale

Charles Gounod wrote the "Pontifical March" in 1869 for the jubilee of Pope Pius IX; it became the anthem of the Holy See and Vatican City in 1949. The Latin words by Raffaello Lavagna, "O felix Roma, o Roma nobilis", praise Rome, seat of Peter.

About this orchestral arrangement

A papal procession: a stately march, an organ-like brass chorale with choir, and timpani and cymbal only at the climaxes.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F major, 4/4, 3:55, 14 parts.

Who wrote Inno e Marcia Pontificale?

The music is by Charles Gounod (1869) and the words by Raffaello Lavagna (Latin, 1991); Antonio Allegra (Italian, 1949). It was adopted 1949.

What inspired the orchestration?

A papal procession: a stately march, an organ-like brass chorale with choir, and timpani and cymbal only at the climaxes.
