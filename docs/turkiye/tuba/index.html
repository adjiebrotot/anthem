About İstiklâl Marşı

Mehmet Âkif Ersoy's poem was chosen by the Grand National Assembly on 12 March 1921, during the War of Independence; Osman Zeki Üngör's music was adopted in 1930. "Korkma!" (Fear not!), it begins: the crimson flag will never fade. The first two stanzas are sung.

About this orchestral arrangement

An Ottoman mehter band march: oboes and bassoons with the reedy colour of the zurna over a strong-weak bass drum and cymbal beat, brass answering, strings from tremolo to a full sweep.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F minor, 4/4, 1:10, 16 parts.

Who wrote İstiklâl Marşı?

The music is by Osman Zeki Üngör (1930) and the words by Mehmet Âkif Ersoy (1921). It was adopted words 12 March 1921, music 1930.
