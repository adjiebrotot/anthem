About O Canada

"O Canada" was first performed in Quebec City on 24 June 1880, with music by Calixa Lavallée and French words by Adolphe-Basile Routhier. Robert Stanley Weir's English words followed in 1908, and the song became the official anthem by the National Anthem Act on 1 July 1980 (English wording updated in 2018 to "in all of us command"). This version plays the English verse, then the French.

About this orchestral arrangement

A northern landscape: open fifths, a high string shimmer and a solo horn call open a pastoral English pass for clarinet and flute. The French pass becomes broad and majestic, with full strings, a brass chorale and choir.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Eb major, 4/4, 3:06, 21 parts.
