About Negaraku

"Negaraku" (My Country) was chosen for the independence of Malaya in 1957. Its melody is that of the Perak state anthem, a tune also known as "Terang Bulan". The words pledge loyalty to the land where "tumpahnya darahku", my blood was spilt, and pray for a united, blessed people under a happy king.

About this orchestral arrangement

From a tropical breeze to the royal court: a lyrical opening for clarinets and strings, then regal brass and trumpets for the closing lines.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F major, 4/4, 1:22, 17 parts.

Who wrote Negaraku?

The music is by the melody of the Perak state anthem "Allah Lanjutkan Usia Sultan" and the words by written for independence (1957). It was adopted 1957.

Is the melody the official one?

Yes. The tune is the official melody, note for note, checked against published scores; only the orchestration is new.
