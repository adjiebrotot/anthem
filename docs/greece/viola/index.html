About Ymnos eis tin Eleftherian

Dionysios Solomos wrote his 158-stanza "Hymn to Liberty" in 1823, during the Greek War of Independence, and Nikolaos Mantzaros set it to music; the first two stanzas became the national anthem in 1865. They greet Liberty, risen from the sacred bones of the Greeks. It is also the anthem of Cyprus.

About this orchestral arrangement

An Aegean hymn: a solemn, light Mediterranean opening of flute and harp, the violins taking up the tune, and a broad close for choir and brass.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F major, 3/4, 1:10, 18 parts.
