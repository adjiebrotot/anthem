About Anthem of Europe (Ode to Joy)

The Anthem of Europe is the "Ode to Joy" theme from the last movement of Beethoven's Ninth Symphony, where it sets Friedrich Schiller's poem about all people becoming brothers. The Council of Europe adopted it in 1972 and the European Union in 1985; it is played without words, in the universal language of music.

About this orchestral arrangement

Beethovenian variations, built the way the Ninth itself introduces the theme: first the cellos and basses alone, then violas with bassoon, then violins with woodwinds in thirds, and finally a march tutti with choir, brass, and the Turkish band colour of bass drum, cymbals and triangle.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. D major, 4/4, 2:11, 21 parts.
