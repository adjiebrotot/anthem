About Amhrán na bhFiann

"A Soldier's Song" was written by Peadar Kearney in 1907 and set to music with Patrick Heeney; it was sung by the rebels of 1916. Its chorus, in Liam Ó Rinn's Irish translation, became the national anthem in 1926. It sings of soldiers who pledged their lives to Ireland.

About this orchestral arrangement

From the harper to the marching host: a solo harp plays the first pass, the old instrument and emblem of Ireland, then the full orchestra marches in with choir, flutes and trumpets.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Bb major, 4/4, 2:04, 20 parts.

The music is by Patrick Heeney and Peadar Kearney (1910) and the words by Peadar Kearney (1907); Irish text Liam Ó Rinn (1923). It was adopted 1926 (the chorus).
