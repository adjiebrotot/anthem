About Himno Nacional Mexicano

Chosen in a national competition, Francisco González Bocanegra's poem and the Catalan-born Jaime Nunó's music were first performed on 15 September 1854; the anthem became official in 1943. "Mexicanos, al grito de guerra" calls the people to defend the homeland at the cry of war.

About this orchestral arrangement

Bright and fiery: trumpets in thirds and sixths for a mariachi colour, a plucked harp and string off-beat pulse, a crackling snare march, a lyrical stanza, and a blazing last chorus.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. C major (C minor / Eb in the stanza's second half), 4/4, 2:13, 18 parts.

Who wrote Himno Nacional Mexicano?

The music is by Jaime Nunó (1854) and the words by Francisco González Bocanegra (1853). It was adopted first performed 1854, official 1943.

What inspired the orchestration?

Bright and fiery: trumpets in thirds and sixths for a mariachi colour, a plucked harp and string off-beat pulse, a crackling snare march, a lyrical stanza, and a blazing last chorus.
