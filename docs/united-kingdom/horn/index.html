About God Save the King

"God Save the King" was first sung in public in London in 1745 and has been the anthem of the United Kingdom by custom ever since; no law made it official. Its tune has travelled the world, from Liechtenstein's anthem to "My Country, 'Tis of Thee" in the United States, and has been set by Beethoven and others. Since 2022 it is sung as "God Save the King".

About this orchestral arrangement

Coronation pomp: a brass fanfare, a stately verse, and a full tutti repeat with choir.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. G major, 3/4, 1:22, 16 parts.
