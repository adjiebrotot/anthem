About Majulah Singapura

Zubir Said wrote "Majulah Singapura" (Onward Singapore) in 1958 for the City Council; it was adopted in 1959 and became the national anthem at independence in 1965. Sung in Malay by law, it calls all Singaporeans to unite and to progress together.

About this orchestral arrangement

Island city lights: a plucked string ostinato, sparkling flutes and clarinets, trumpets and horns for the repeat, and a crisp brass finale.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. F major, 4/4, 1:10, 18 parts.
