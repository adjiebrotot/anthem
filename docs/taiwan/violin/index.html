About Sanmin Zhuyi

The words come from a 1924 speech by Sun Yat-sen at the Whampoa Military Academy; Cheng Maoyun's music won a 1928 competition. The song became the national anthem of the Republic of China in 1937 and is the anthem used in Taiwan today. It begins with the Three Principles of the People as the nation's foundation.

About this orchestral arrangement

An ancestral hall: solemn and dignified, with a low-string and brass chorale, timpani rolls and a choir.

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. C major, 4/4, 1:42, 17 parts.

Who wrote Sanmin Zhuyi?

The music is by Cheng Maoyun (1928) and the words by Sun Yat-sen (1924). It was adopted 1930 (provisional), 1937 (official).

What inspired the orchestration?

An ancestral hall: solemn and dignified, with a low-string and brass chorale, timpani rolls and a choir.
