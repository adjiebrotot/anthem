About The Star-Spangled Banner

Francis Scott Key wrote the words in September 1814 after watching the British bombardment of Fort McHenry in Baltimore, and seeing the flag still flying at dawn. Sung to John Stafford Smith's tune "To Anacreon in Heaven", it became the national anthem by law on 3 March 1931.

About this orchestral arrangement

Fireworks: a snare roll and trumpet fanfare, a lyrical first half for horns and strings, timpani and cymbals on "the rockets' red glare", and a held high note under a trumpet descant on "free".

The melody is the official tune, note for note; only the orchestration is new. Ab major, 3/4, 1:32, 16 parts.
