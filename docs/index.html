Questions

What licence are the arrangements under?

It depends on the melody. Most national anthems are in the public domain: their arrangements here, with the sheet music and MIDI files, are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, free for any use, commercial too, with credit and adaptations shared alike. Where the melody is still in copyright, it belongs to its owner and its use follows their rules (usually fine at meetings, education, ceremonies and community events, never in a defaming context or commercially without their licence); the arrangement itself asks only for credit (CC BY 4.0).

Which anthems here are still in copyright?

ASEAN, Bahrain, Brunei, Iran, Laos, Mongolia, North Korea, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, South Africa, South Korea, Taiwan (Republic of China), Thailand, Türkiye, UAE, Vietnam. Each of their pages names the owner where it is known.

How do I credit an arrangement?

Name the anthem, the arranger and the site, and the licence: "Advance Australia Fair", arranged for orchestra by adjiebrotot (anthem.adjiebrotots.com), CC BY-SA 4.0. In a concert programme, a video description or the credits of a recording, that one line is enough.
